an experiment in spatial sound

The Horse Below

A horse, stranded on a planet that is entirely underwater. Ten places hum, ring and breathe around you. Walk, and listen.

🎧 Headphones strongly recommended: sounds are placed in space around the horse.

WASD / ↑←↓→ move · Ctrl+D opens the audio editor · Esc closes it

Controls

WASD / arrow keys: move the horse
Ctrl+D (or the Audio editor button): assign your own sounds
Esc: close this / the editor

Spatial sounds use HRTF rendering for headphones. It is an approximation, not a true binaural recording.

Audio editor

Assignments are saved in this browser (IndexedDB). They are not part of the GitHub repo; use Export to back them up.
